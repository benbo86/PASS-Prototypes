Group messaging research

What customers are asking for when they request group messages: conversations with several carers where everyone can reply, and replies to broadcasts.

Support cases

Salesforce cases asking for group messaging, found by searching case subjects and text on 19 November 2026. Nine requests from six customers, all raised since 15 October 2026, the weeks after Two-Way Messaging was released. All are still "New Request". Together the six customers pay £8,078.25 MRR (Total Contracted MRR from Salesforce, each customer counted once).

What we've heard so far

  • Broadcasts can't be replied to, and that's the most common complaintFour cases say it directly. Customers want to send one message to several carers and have everyone reply in the same thread, as with a group chat.
  • Some want group chat to replace WhatsAppTwo customers say they run package or team groups on WhatsApp today, and a third wants fewer external messaging apps. Their reasons are governance, information on personal devices, and communication records spread across systems. Others only want the convenience of one message and one thread.
  • Groups by package, area or tagOne customer wants groups built from care packages, areas or custom tags. Others only talk about selecting several recipients when starting a conversation.
  • Beyond textTwo customers also ask for file and image sharing in groups. This is covered by a separate ticket, so it isn't part of this one. One also asks for read status and acknowledgements to carry across, and another for an office-level on/off setting for group chats.

Four cases from one customer over a month. They can broadcast to a group, but recipients can't reply, so conversations need separate threads.   • Bulk and group email or SMS.Seren Support Services asked to send emails to tagged groups from the roster (case 00161244, September 2024, open), and for a bulk email and SMS broadcast by region and role (case 00198008, November 2025, marked Released).
  • A different question, not a request.Bluebird Care Southampton asked why a message to one person, or a broadcast to a few people, seemed visible to everyone (case 00224058, still waiting on the customer). It points to the same area of the product, but isn't asking for group chat.

What this means for the prototypes

  • Two different needs sit under one phrase.Replies to a broadcast, where one sender writes to many and the replies stay with the message, and a true group chat, where everyone writes to everyone. The cases use both words for both.
  • Broadcasts can't be replied to by design today.AIOP-23550 built broadcast threads as read-only with a "can't reply" notice. Changing that is the smallest step towards what customers describe.
  • Not yet known.Whether customers would be content with replies to broadcasts, or really need group chat; how groups should be formed (picked per message, by package, by area or tag); and whether replies should be visible to all recipients or only to the sender.

Summarised from case descriptions written by our support team, not from conversations with the customers. We haven't searched Intercom conversations or calls.

Existing work

Searched Jira (AIOP) on 19 November 2026. There is no ticket for group conversations or replies to broadcasts.

  • Nothing covers group chat or replying to broadcasts.Of about 40 messaging tickets, none does. A ticket with unusual wording could have been missed.
  • Broadcasts are designed as read-only.AIOP-23550 (Accepted) built the read-only broadcast thread with a "can't reply" notice.
  • Nearby, not the same.AIOP-25608 "[PRODUCT TBC] Area access in messaging" (Backlog) is about who sees what by area. AIOP-25209 and AIOP-25208 (Backlog) let managers message carers and other managers from the app.
