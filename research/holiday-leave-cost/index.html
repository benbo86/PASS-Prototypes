How other products work out what a leave request costs

PASS doesn't know which days a carer would have worked, so the app no longer shows a request's cost: the office confirms it on approval. This page looks at how comparable HR, rota and home-care products handle the same problem, to inform whether we could show an estimate.

Researched 26 October 2026 from public help centres, not hands-on use. Linked from Holidays (mobile app) and Holiday Requests (web).

1From a set working pattern

Each employee has a pattern of days and hours. A request only counts the days they normally work, so the exact cost is known at request time.

BrightHR

Checks the employee's working pattern and records only the time they'd normally work. Someone on Monday, Wednesday and Friday who books Monday to Friday is charged 3 days, not 5. (Example from the Australian help centre; UK behaviour not separately documented.)

Breathe

A working pattern is required for every employee. Leave already booked is recalculated if the pattern changes.

“…so the system knows how many hours a week they work and on what days.”

For flexible or zero-hours staff, the admin ticks “ignore working pattern” and the employee types the hours on each request.

Sage HR

Working-pattern policies deduct either whole days or exact working hours, depending on how the policy is set up.

2From the rota

The rota decides which days count.

RotaCloud

Days marked non-working on the rota aren't deducted. For staff on an hours allowance, the employee can suggest hours on the request (daily or a total), and the approver can edit them before approving. This is close to our hours-scheme form.

Blocking: day allowances always block a request over the balance; hour allowances only block when the balance is zero or below — the same rule we chose.

3From averages

Closest to PASS, which already uses a 52-week average for holiday pay.

Deputy

Works out an average working day from the last 52 weeks of paid work (up to 104 if needed), starting after 5 approved timesheets. Managers see the figure on the request but can't edit it.

“The amount shown when the request is approved is an estimate until the day the leave is taken.”

UK rules apply the average on the first day of leave, so it can change between approval and the holiday.

Birdie Home-care competitor

Same 12.07% accrual as PASS. Each day of holiday is charged at the carer's rolling average hours per day. For bookings of 7 days or more, it uses the carer's average days worked per week, so it never needs to know which particular days they'd have worked. Part days count as full days.

Not stated: how a short booking that includes a day off is handled.

Nothing public was found for Nourish or CareLineLive.

What this means for PASS

  • No one gets an exact figure without a working pattern.Without one, products either use averages or let a person enter the amount. That supports the current approach of the office confirming the amount.
  • Birdie's 7-day rule is worth exploring in discovery.PASS already works out average daily hours for holiday pay. Average days per week would give a reasonable estimate for holidays of a week or more, without the rota or a pattern. Short bookings are the hard part.
  • If we show an estimate, call it one.Deputy labels its figure an estimate even though it's calculated from data.
  • Ticked working days match the market norm.The planned change to the contract's days-per-week option (ticking which days someone works) is how BrightHR, Breathe and Sage HR solve it.
