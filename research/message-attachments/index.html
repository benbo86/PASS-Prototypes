What we've heard so far

  • Carers sending photos to the office is wantedThree customers asked for it and nobody was against it. The fourth sees it as a nice-to-have.
  • Medication photos come up mostThree customers raised a carer photographing a medication box or label. The office can read a photo at a glance when printed instructions are unclear. One customer sees this as clearly valuable; another already handles it through Tasks.
  • Customers split on the office sending attachments to carersTwo want it, for files, simple instructions and confidential documents. Two see little value, because official documents are still handed over physically.
  • Value depends on what's already in useOne customer sees little value in attachments overall, because visit photos go through care notes and medication changes through Tasks.

Sessions

Here to Care

8 July 2026

Third customer to raise the medication photo case.

Carer to office
  • A carer looking at a medication box often doesn't quite grasp the printed instructions, but the office can interpret it instantly from a photo.
  • They see this as clearly valuable, not just a nice-to-have.
Office to carer
  • Wanted for simple instructions (for example, "empty the bin") and for confidential documents.

SOS Homecare

7 July 2026

Sees little value in attachments overall.

Carer to office
  • Most photo-worthy content, such as client visit photos, already goes through care notes.
  • A nice-to-have for photos of medication changes (a new prescription or box). They handle that through Tasks today, so it isn't essential.
Office to carer
  • Not needed.

Arch Care Services

3 July 2026

Carer to office
  • Valuable, for mileage claims and handwritten notes sent as photos.
Office to carer
  • Low value. Official documents are still handed over physically, not scanned or sent digitally.

Support cases

Salesforce cases searched on 6 November 2026 for requests to send photos or files in messaging. Only cases about staff messaging are listed here.

Palm 2 Palm Case 00223202 · October 2026

Asks for photo and image attachments in two-way messaging. A carer may need to share an image with the office as part of a conversation, and would find it useful to do that in messaging instead of another platform. They expect it would make messaging more versatile and reduce the need for external messaging apps for work conversations.

Searched case subjects only, because a case's description can't be filtered. Cases about the customer Communications tab (a different feature) also ask for images and are not counted here.

What this means for the ticket

  • Start with carers sending photos to the office.Customers agree on this direction, a recent case matches it, and the medication photo case is the clearest use.
  • Leave office to carer attachments out for now.Customers are split two against two, and there's no support case for it.
  • Questions still open.Safeguarding and GDPR for photos of customers, whether messaging or Tasks is the right place, and size limits. Linked from AIOP-25481.
